Tank Tactics

How to play

This game can get personal. Play with people who'll still be your friends afterwards.

The goal

Be the last tank standing.

The board

  • Everyone has one tank on a square grid. Only one tank fits on a tile.
  • Everyone can always see every tank's position, HP (health) and range.
  • Range is measured in a straight line from tile centre to tile centre. A tank 3 across is at distance 3, but a tank 2 across and 2 down is at distance 2.8, so range 2 doesn't reach it. When you select a tank, the board shades the tiles it can reach.
  • Moving is one step to any of the 8 surrounding tiles, diagonals included.
  • There are no walls and no line of sight. You can shoot "through" other tanks. Range is the only limit.

Your tank

You start with 3 HP, 1 AP (action point) and range 3. The host may have changed these numbers; the game page shows the real values.

Action points

  • There are no turns. Every living tank gets +1 AP every 24 hours, all at the same moment. That is the default: the host can pick another schedule, such as every weekday at 09:00, every 6 hours or the last Friday of each month, in their time zone. AP always arrives on a whole minute, at most once a minute and at least once a month. The game page shows the schedule and when the next AP arrives.
  • Spend AP whenever you like, as much as you have, in any order. Unspent AP is kept. Saving up is a legitimate strategy.

Actions

"Within range" means within your range.

Destroying tanks

  • A tank at 0 HP is destroyed and taken off the board.
  • Loot: whoever fires the destroying shot takes all of the victim's AP.
  • If your tank is destroyed you can still watch the game and use the public chat. You can no longer send or receive private messages.

Talking

There is a public chat and private messages between any two players. Deals, threats and betrayals all happen here. Nothing stops you from shooting someone who just gave you AP, and nothing stops them from doing it to you.

Optional rules (the host chooses)

The jury (off by default)

If the jury is on, destroyed players become jurors. Each AP tick closes a jury cycle (so with the default schedule, once a day). During a cycle each juror can vote for one living player, and can change that vote until the cycle closes. The cycle closes just after that tick’s AP is handed out. The living player with the most votes gets +1 AP. If players tie, they all get it (or, if the host chose, one of them at random). With no votes, nobody gets anything. Votes are public unless the host made them secret.

So be nice to the people you kill: they vote.

Fog of war (off by default)

  • Off: everything is public, including everyone's AP and every gift.
  • Alliance: you see only your own AP. A gift of AP or HP is seen only by the giver and the receiver; nobody else learns it happened. HP changes on the board can still give a gift of HP away. When a tank is destroyed, everyone sees the kill, but only the killer sees how much AP they looted.

Positions, HP, range, shots, moves, kills, range upgrades and the winner are always public.

Other host settings

The host can also change the board size, starting stats, when AP arrives (the schedule), the upgrade cost, an AP cap or a maximum range, and whether destroyed tanks leave wrecks that block their tile. The host can also turn off giving HP or looting, and set a time limit. The time limit ends the game on the next whole minute after it runs out, and no AP arrives at that moment. When it is reached, the living tank with the most HP wins. Ties go to the most AP, then the highest range. If still tied, the win is shared.

Winning

The game ends when exactly one tank is left. That player wins.

# Tank Tactics: how to play

**This game can get personal. Play with people who'll still be your friends afterwards.**

## The goal

Be the **last tank standing**.

## The board

- Everyone has one tank on a square grid. Only one tank fits on a tile.
- Everyone can always see every tank's **position, HP (health) and range**.
- **Range** is measured in a straight line from tile centre to tile centre. A tank 3
  across is at distance 3, but a tank 2 across and 2 down is at distance 2.8, so range 2
  doesn't reach it. When you select a tank, the board shades the tiles it can reach.
- **Moving** is one step up, down, left or right. No diagonal moves.
- There are no walls and no line of sight. You can shoot "through" other tanks.
  **Range is the only limit.**

## Your tank

You start with **3 HP**, **0 AP** (action points) and **range 3**. The host may have
changed these numbers; the game page shows the real values.

## Action points

- There are **no turns**. Every living tank gets **+1 AP every 24 hours**, all at the
  same moment. That is the default: the host can pick another schedule, such as every
  weekday at 09:00, every 6 hours or the last Friday of each month, in their time zone.
  Times follow that zone's clock, so "09:00" stays 09:00 when the clocks change. AP
  always arrives on a whole minute, at most once a minute, and at least once every
  month. The game page shows the schedule and when the next AP arrives.
- Spend AP whenever you like, as much as you have, in any order. Unspent AP is kept.
  Saving up is a legitimate strategy.

## Actions

| Action | Cost | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Move** | 1 AP | Move one tile in any of the 8 directions, onto an empty tile. |
| **Shoot** | 1 AP | Deal 1 damage to any tank within your range. Allies too. |
| **Give AP** | the AP you give | Hand any amount of your AP to a tank within your range. |
| **Give HP** | the HP you give | Hand some of your HP to a tank within your range. You must keep at least 1. |
| **Upgrade range** | 3 AP | Your range goes up by 1, permanently. |

"Within range" means within **your** range.

## Destroying tanks

- A tank at **0 HP is destroyed** and taken off the board.
- **Loot (off by default):** if the host turns it on, whoever fires the destroying shot
  takes **all of the victim's AP**.
- If your tank is destroyed you can still watch the game and use the public chat.
  You can no longer send or receive private messages.

## Talking

There is a public chat and private messages between any two players. Deals, threats and
betrayals all happen here. Nothing stops you from shooting someone who just gave you AP,
and nothing stops them from doing it to you.

## Optional rules (the host chooses)

### The jury (on by default)

While the jury is on, destroyed players become **jurors**. Each AP tick closes a jury
cycle, so with the default schedule that's once a day. During a cycle each juror can vote
for one living player, and can change that vote until the cycle closes. The cycle closes
just after that tick's AP is handed out. The living player
with the most votes gets **+1 AP**. If players tie, they all get it (or, if the host chose,
one of them at random). With no votes, nobody gets anything. Votes are public unless the
host made them secret.

So be nice to the people you kill: they vote.

### Fog of war (off by default)

- **Off:** everything is public, including everyone's AP and every gift.
- **Alliance:** you see only **your own** AP. A gift of AP or HP is seen only by the
  giver and the receiver; nobody else learns it happened. HP changes on the board can
  still give a gift of HP away. When a tank is destroyed, everyone sees the kill, but
  only the killer sees how much AP they looted.

Positions, HP, range, shots, moves, kills, range upgrades and the winner are always public.

### Other host settings

The host can also change the board size, starting stats, the AP schedule, the
upgrade cost, an AP cap or a maximum range, and whether destroyed tanks leave wrecks that
block their tile. The host can also turn off giving HP or the jury, turn on looting, and set a time limit
in days.
The time limit ends the game on the next whole minute after it runs out, and no
AP arrives at that moment. When it is reached, the living tank with the most HP wins. Ties go to the most
AP, then the highest range. If still tied, the win is shared.

## Winning

The game ends when exactly one tank is left. That player wins.
